## Phase Goal

**As a** Mac user, **I want to** group windows from any apps into one tabbed tile, **so that** related windows share space without overlapping.

<objective>
UI-SPEC Surface 6 (Claude's discretion, resolved by the approved UI-SPEC): groups on the zoom stage and in the overview. The engine adds strip moves to camera moves and keeps hidden members out of every stage tile; the shell draws an icon-only strip layer, hides the real strip panels during moves, labels collapsed groups and counts groups once in the overview.

Purpose: with zoom on, the stage (a `.floating` panel) covers the real `.normal`-level strip; without a stage strip a group would look like a plain window and hidden members could leak into snapshots.

Output: `StripMove`, `CameraMove.strips`, `StripLayerStyle` + strip layer, panel hide/reveal, collapsed-group label, overview changes, background capture after a switch.
</objective>
