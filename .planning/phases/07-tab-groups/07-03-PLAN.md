## Phase Goal

**As a** Mac user, **I want to** group windows from any apps into one tabbed tile, **so that** related windows share space without overlapping.

<objective>
Completes the tab strip so it behaves like part of the group: exact Surface 2 geometry (titles, icon-only, overflow menu, narrow strips), hover/press/contrast styling, VoiceOver structure, following the selected window during drags and app self-moves, correct ordering, and correct Space behaviour (verified by a VM test, per the planning brief).

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 1 requires the strip to follow the group as it moves and resizes; Pitfall 8 (Space stranding) and the overflow rules are the strip's main failure modes.

Output: `TabStripLayout` + tests, extended TabStripPanels/TabCopy, follow and backstop wiring in ArrangeController, `.raiseStrip` on member focus, `TabStripSpaceIntegrationTests`.
</objective>
