## Phase Goal

**As a** Mac user, **I want to** grant Pane access and have it track and move every window, **so that** no slow app or lock screen can freeze or lose them.

<objective>
Close 02-14 SUMMARY known limit 3, the "race without Space data" (WIN-06, WIN-09), using only public API, per the project lead's decision of 2026-10-25. Without SkyLight Space data, a Space switch in the same update as the close of a whole tab group can show an untracked same-frame twin from the other Space. That twin passes the former-tab evidence rule (it was offscreen in the group's slot at the last refresh) and inherits the group's identity. Phase 3 would then give the closed group's tile to an unrelated window.

The fix: AXWindowSystem observes `NSWorkspace.activeSpaceDidChangeNotification` and passes a "Space changed since this app's last update" Bool into the engine through `ObservationContext`. Only in fallback mode (no Space data) and only in a flagged update, TabGroupTracker refuses the orphan handoff and the hidden-tab fold, and the fold guard holds. The window is decided at the next update instead. Its evidence is then gone: the twin was onscreen in that update, and a window left on the other Space was not listed. With Space data, behaviour is unchanged.

Purpose: a closed tab group must never pass its identity to a window on another Space. That is a lost window in the phase goal's sense, because the user's layout slot would follow the wrong window.
Output: `ObservationContext.spaceChanged`, the refusal in TabGroupTracker and the fold-guard hold in WindowTracker, the fake's `showSpace(_:notifies:)`, the live observer in AXWindowSystem, and scenario, unit and fold-guard tests.

How the lead decision is read (record this in the SUMMARY): "the hidden-tab fold or merge" means one rule, a tracked standalone window taken as a hidden tab of a group. That is how the Merge All Windows case is named in the 02-14 tests (`mergingTwoTrackedWindowsLeavesOneGroupAndReportsTheOtherGone`). The room-rule join (a listed tab joins the group whose own members it finds in its slot) is not refused. It never takes a tracked standalone window and never passes on a closed group's identity. Its evidence is the same at the next update, so refusing it for one update would change nothing. Refusing it by starting a new group would give one tab group two identities. The two-same-frame-groups case it can merge is the accepted single-Space fallback override in 02-VERIFICATION. The Cmd-T opener is also still taken in a flagged update. Its evidence, a shown tab absent from the previous CG list, cannot come from a Space switch.
</objective>

Out of scope, and not to be built here: the Phase 6 co-visibility window-to-Space map, which builds on the same notification (ROADMAP Phase 6 scope notes). This plan only marks updates; it keeps no Space history.

Facts that constrain the design:
- Updates are per app (`WindowTracker.ingest(_:context:)` per AppObservation). A Space switch must therefore mark every app, and each app's next full update consumes its own mark.
- TabGroupTracker re-resolves groups only on a full AXWindows answer (`app.listFailure == nil` in WindowTracker.ingest), and `lastCG` / `lastListed` move only then. So a mark is consumed only by a full answer; a failed list keeps it pending.
- After a flagged update, the race's evidence is gone. The twin was onscreen in that update's CG list, so the handoff's "offscreen in the slot last refresh" test fails at the next update. A window left behind on the other Space was not listed in it, so `canBeHiddenTab` rejects it as a tracked standalone window not listed last refresh.
- An app untouched by the switch may wait for the 5 s sweep, so its mark can stay pending for a few seconds. Every rule refused under the mark must therefore be safe to defer for one update.
