## Phase Goal

**As a** Mac user, **I want to** grant Pane access and have it track and move every window, **so that** no slow app or lock screen can freeze or lose them.

<objective>
Close verification gaps 1 and 2 (WIN-06, WIN-09). A native tab group must never take a same-frame window of the same app on another Space as its hidden tab, and a wrong fold must never report a live window destroyed. Per the project lead's decision of 2026-10-25, a read-only SkyLight Spaces lookup is pulled forward from Phase 6 into its own PanePrivate file. It is registered as a new capability that can be forced off, and when it is off Pane falls back to single-Space behaviour with the previous-refresh CG-list rule.

Purpose: Same-frame windows of one app on different Spaces are the normal case in a tiling manager. Today TabGroupTracker can track one window twice, report a live window destroyed, or merge two groups for good (02-VERIFICATION gaps 1 and 2; REVIEW iteration 3 CR-01).
Output: SkyLightSpaces + Capability.spaces, Space-aware TabGroupTracker, a fold guard in WindowTracker, Space modelling in FakeWindowSystem, the reviewer's C/S scenarios as committed tests, a live lookup test, the Debug menu line, and the ROADMAP Phase 6 note.
</objective>
