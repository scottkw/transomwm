## Phase Goal

**As a** multi-display user, **I want to** see every window on every display and Space in one overview, **so that** I can jump anywhere and keep each layout.

<objective>

Purpose: OVER-04 says each Space keeps its own layout. A ghost window kept as alive holds a tile open on its Space, and the lookup-off fallback must never invent desks. These are the two ways a per-Space layout goes wrong today.

Output: CloseRule G-03-9 rows, WindowTracker input, `CoVisibilitySpaces` (Debug-only, RESEARCH Pattern 8 scope), the Debug Spaces lines, and the first `OverviewCopy` strings.
