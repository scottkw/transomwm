    - App quit, then `.appLaunched` for that bundle at t: the deadline becomes t + 10 s; each later `.windowCreated` of that bundle moves it to that window's time + 10 s; with no match it expires then.
    - Transom launch, docked saved window unmatched, its app not running: placeholder with deadline restore + 5 min; if the app launches later, the 10 s sliding rule takes over.
    - Transom launch, docked saved window unmatched, its app running (has tracked windows): deadline restore + 10 s, extended to each new window of that bundle + 10 s.
    - Transom launch, floating saved window unmatched: kept in its Desktop layer with the same deadline, then dropped at expiry; nothing reserves a tile for it.

    - Inventories with a CG list that lacks the placeholder's window ID never close it.

## Trust Boundaries
