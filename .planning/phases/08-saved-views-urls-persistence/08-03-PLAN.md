## Phase Goal

**As a** power user, **I want to** save views to hotkeys and run Transom from transom:// URLs, **so that** my layouts and automations survive restarts.

<objective>
The persistence tracer for saved views: a view saved before a Transom relaunch still lands on its windows after the relaunch, after a split and a move. Then identity remapping (rekeys and relaunch matches) and Space-UUID rekeying of view desks, and finally going to a view on another Space or display with the shell's feedback (joined announcement, toast display, Debug line).

Purpose: VIEW-02 and the D-03 half of VIEW-01. Placeholder fills (08-04) call the same remap helper.

Output: `LayoutFile.views`, views in `snapshot` and `.restore`, `remapViews`, view desks in `SpaceRekey`, the go-view switch flow in `ArrangeController`, `ViewCopy` Debug line, `ViewsIntegrationTests`.
</objective>
