## Phase Goal

**As a** user, **I want to** have docked windows fill the whole screen and floating windows sit on top of them like normal macOS windows, **so that** I never have to learn or manage a "Desktop tile".

<objective>
Make every keyboard and menu command work on a desk with no Desktop tile and no tree, and put every frame that used to be clamped or centred into the Desktop rect on the visible frame instead (UI-SPEC substitution rule).

Purpose: RESEARCH found a dozen places that compile without a Desktop leaf but beep, float or throw `nodeNotFound(n0)` at runtime (Pitfall 3). They are fixed here, with tests on explicitly empty desks, before 08.1.1-09 makes every new desk empty.

Output: autoTile, Dock, Arrange All and Split with Finder on an empty desk; snap, Float, Float All, Move Out of Group, tear-off, Float Group and dissolve on the visible frame; `showingDesktop()` and `tearOffDesktop` deleted.
</objective>
