### WR-01: A torn-off tab over its own docked group groups with a floater hidden behind that group

**Files modified:** `Sources/TransomEngine/Arranger.swift`, `Tests/TransomEngineTests/ArrangerTabsTests.swift`
**Commit:** 0249086
**Applied fix:** The `beneath` own-tile exclusion in `hover(for:)` now requires `docked`, so it matches the `switch` just below it. It applies only to a title-bar drag of a docked window. During a tear-off the group's tile stays on screen, so it remains `beneath`, and the focus-recency check in `floatingGroupTarget` runs again. The comment was updated to say this. Added the repro test `aTornTabOverItsOwnGroupDoesNotGroupWithAFloaterBehindTheGroup` to `ArrangerTearOffReleaseTests`. It sets up `[b, f]` docked with `f` selected, puts `t` floating inside the group's tile, focuses `t` then `f`, then tears `b` onto `t`'s centre, holds for the group dwell and releases. It expects no Group preview and no floating group. To support the test, `bareGroup` gained an optional `floating:` parameter that adds extra windows. The test failed before the fix (2 issues: a Group preview, and a floating group was formed) and passes after it.
