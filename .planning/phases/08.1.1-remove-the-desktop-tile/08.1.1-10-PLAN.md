## Phase Goal

**As a** user, **I want to** have docked windows fill the whole screen and floating windows sit on top of them like normal macOS windows, **so that** I never have to learn or manage a "Desktop tile".

<objective>
Bring the live (VM-only) integration suites into line with the empty desk 08.1.1-09 made the default: convert their setups, their Desktop-share expectations and the lines that read a plan's Desktop rect, and prove one suite end to end in the VM.

Purpose: the integration target compiles on the host but runs only in the VM, so the flip left it compiling and stale. Converting it here, before the deletion plans, means 08.1.1-12 (plan types) and 08.1.1-14 (node kind) hit no integration reader, and 08.1.1-16's VM runs test the new rules instead of failing on old oracles.

Output: seven integration files converted; FourthsIntegrationTests run on macOS 27.2.
</objective>
