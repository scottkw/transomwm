## Phase Goal

**As a** user, **I want to** have docked windows fill the whole screen and floating windows sit on top of them like normal macOS windows, **so that** I never have to learn or manage a "Desktop tile".

<objective>
Make drags behave as UI-SPEC Surface 2's changed rows say: no clamp ghost and no clamp for floating windows, ⇧ and Hold ⌥ pull-outs never refused, an empty desk that docks only from its 24 pt edge band, and a front floating window that wins the group dwell.

Purpose: these are the drag sites RESEARCH found silently relying on the Desktop tile (Pitfalls 3–5). They change unconditionally, while new desks still carry a Desktop tile, so the flip in 08.1.1-09 only has Desktop-share expectations left to re-derive.

Output: simpler `hover`, `decide`, `commitUndock` and `missed`; `Hover.crossesTiles`, `Arranger.crosses` and the hidden-Desktop snap-back label removed; tests.
</objective>
