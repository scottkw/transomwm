## Phase Goal

**As a** user, **I want to** have docked windows fill the whole screen and floating windows sit on top of them like normal macOS windows, **so that** I never have to learn or manage a "Desktop tile".

<objective>
Start closing the first host-UAT gap (8.1.1 D-16): every docking position must give a window exactly its part of the screen, and the rest may stay empty. The layout tree cannot express "empty" today. Every split's children share the whole frame, so a lone half grows to fill the desk.

Investigation (planner, 2026-11-06), and why this mechanism:
- Phase 8 waiting tiles (`Placeholder`, Arranger+Placeholders.swift) were considered and rejected. They are untracked window refs with deadlines (5 min at most), they draw a visible waiting tile, and each belongs to one app. Empty space has no deadline, no window and no drawing.
- An extent or margin per desk (the tree resolved inside a sub-rect) was rejected. It can't hold an empty gap between two windows ("Left half, then 4th column" must leave the 3rd column empty), and it can't hold the empty quarters around a corner (D-17).
- Explicit weights at the root with a missing side was rejected for the same reason: corners and cross-axis docks need empty space below the root.
- The smallest correct mechanism is one more leaf kind, `Node.spacer`: no window, always takes part in `resolve`, never drawn, never a focus, swap or group target. Every invariant (weights sum to 1, validate, normalise, undo snapshots) keeps working.

This plan asks the user to confirm that one-way choice (it changes layout.json) and the rules that follow from it, then builds the model side: the leaf, its shape rules, resolve, persistence at layout.json schema 5, and the edits later plans use (vacate, fill, the leave rule). No command creates an empty area yet. Plan 21 changes the docking arithmetic.

Purpose: the foundation for D-16 and D-17. Without a way to hold empty space, no docking position can be exact.
Output: `Node.spacer`, its validate and normalise rules, `LayoutTree.vacate(_:)` and `fill(_:with:states:)`, layout.json schema 5, EmptyAreaTests.
</objective>

## Empty-area rules (the checkpoint confirms these; plans 21-27 cite them as E1-E7)

- **E1 Exact.** A docking position (half, third, fourth, corner) gives the window exactly that part of the visible frame. Its label always names that position. There are no more grown labels such as "Whole desk" or "Left two thirds".
- **E2 Empty first.** Empty space under the new position is used first. Docked windows on a side move into the empty space on their side before any of them shrinks. When that runs out they shrink proportionally, as today.
- **E3 Nothing grows.** A side with no docked window stays empty, and so does space a docked window leaves while moving aside. No docked window grows because another window docked to a position.
- **E4 Moving leaves a hole.** A docked window moved to a position leaves its old place empty.
- **E5 Leaving.** A docked window that closes, floats or is dragged away gives its space to an empty area beside it, if there is one; otherwise its neighbours share it as today. A desk with only empty areas left is an empty desk (nothing drawn, D-07).
- **E6 Filling.** Over an empty area a drag shows nothing and just moves the window, except within 24 pt of the screen edge, where it previews that empty area and docks into it. Dock Where It Is over an empty area fills it. New Windows: Tile, Arrange All and Split with New Finder Window with nothing docked focused fill the largest empty area first. On an empty desk all three still fill the screen (D-08).
- **E7 Inert.** Focus, Move Window, swap and zoom steps never land on an empty area. A gap handle between a window and an empty area resizes the window into it. Balance, Rotate and Flip treat an empty area like any child. Minimising keeps today's behaviour: its siblings, empty areas included, share its space until it returns.
