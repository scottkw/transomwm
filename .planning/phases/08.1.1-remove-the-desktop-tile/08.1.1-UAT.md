## User notes
- 2026-11-06, ISSUE (major): with three floating windows and nothing docked, docking one to the Left half made it fill the whole screen. That is the D-04 / 08.1 D-14 "grow into an empty side" rule, but with no Desktop tile an empty desk is now normal, so it surprises. User decision: "Take only that half" — a half/third/fourth on a desk with nothing docked on that side keeps its span and leaves the rest empty (floating windows show there) until another window docks into it. Gap closure needed (changes D-04 / 08.1 D-14).
- 2026-11-06: user asked why support VoiceOver at all. Answer given: native menus get it for free; keep that, but no custom VoiceOver work for overlays/previews.
- 2026-11-06, DECISION (widens the issue above): "All screen portions designated by the app should be a portion of the screen irrespective of other windows." Every docking position (halves, thirds, fourths, and the new corners) gives exactly that part of the visible frame, whatever else is docked; nothing grows into an empty side. Supersedes 08.1 D-14's grow rule and 08.1.1 D-04.
- 2026-11-06, ISSUE (gap): the Dock Window menu has no corner quarters. User wants Top Left / Top Right / Bottom Left / Bottom Right as docking positions (menu, rebindable hotkeys, transom:// and CLI), each exactly that quarter of the screen. Today the ⌃⌥⌘U/I/J/K corners only snap floating windows.
