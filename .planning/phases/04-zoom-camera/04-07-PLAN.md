## Phase Goal

**As a** Mac user, **I want to** zoom out to see every window and zoom back in to any of them, **so that** I can find and focus any window fast.

<objective>
ZOOM-13 end to end: with zoom on, a drop or keyboard Dock/Move that does not fit is accepted, the windows that lose room become collapsed app-colour blocks in their tiles (drawn by small persistent panels at rest and by the stage during moves), and double-clicking a block zooms in to it.

Purpose: success criterion 6's last clause, and D-A's "with zoom on, a drop that doesn't fit at the home view is accepted".

Output: collapse acceptance in the Arranger, the `.collapsedBlocks` effect, CollapsedPanels, the Collapsed drop-preview state, collapsed stage tiles.

Design:
- `CameraState.collapsible` (declared in 04-02) is the only new state: identities the user accepted as collapsed by a drop or keyboard Dock/Move. 04-02's ViewPlanner already gives them block rects and parks them; this plan makes the home plan use it too (`planFloatingLostRoom` floats only unfit windows outside the set) and feeds the set.
- Session-only on purpose: layout.json stays schema 1, and a relaunch floats them like Phase 3 lost room (safe on recovery launches, RESEARCH Pitfall 11).
- `.collapsedBlocks([CollapsedBlock], displayUUID:)` mirrors `.gapHandles`: sent per display only when the list changes.
</objective>
