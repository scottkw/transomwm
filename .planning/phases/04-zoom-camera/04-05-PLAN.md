## Phase Goal

**As a** Mac user, **I want to** zoom out to see every window and zoom back in to any of them, **so that** I can find and focus any window fast.

<objective>
Make every zoom command reachable and rebindable: 11 hotkeys, 11 URLs, the status menu's Zoom section with the on/off toggle and the Screen Recording recovery item, Debug lines, and the Shortcuts window with a Recorder row for every hotkey.

Purpose: ZOOM-08 (rebindable), the hotkey/URL halves of ZOOM-09/10/11, and success criterion 5's "turning zoom off leaves Phase 3 tiling fully working".

Output: extended Hotkeys, TransomURL, CommandMenu, StatusItemController, ZoomCopy; a new ShortcutsWindowController.

Design:
- `ArrangeController.dispatch` turns `.camera(c)` into `ArrangerInput.camera(c, displayUUID:, focused:, pointer:)` using the display under `NSEvent.mouseLocation` (converted to top-left global points). Hotkeys, menu items and URLs all act on the pointer's display (UI-SPEC: URL commands act on the display under the pointer; the menu opens on the pointer's display).
- Existing HotkeyTableTests expectations change in exactly three sanctioned ways, all from locked decisions: the 33-entry count and lists grow to 44 (UI-SPEC Surface 8), and ⌃⌥⌘, / ⌃⌥⌘. leave the "reserved by macOS Accessibility" list because CONTEXT C locks them for step out / in (UI-SPEC "Known overlap, kept by decision"; those macOS shortcuts are off by default and Transom logs when they are on). The Phase 3 33 names keep their own assertion.
- The VoiceOver letter-chord fallback the UI-SPEC mentions extends a Phase 3 fallback that does not exist in the code (`grep -rn VoiceOver Sources/TransomMac/Input` finds nothing), so there is nothing to extend.
</objective>
