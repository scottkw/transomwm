## Phase Goal

**As a** Mac user, **I want to** drag windows into nested tiles that reflow as windows open and close, **so that** my windows never overlap.

<objective>
Close the Desktop-minimum part of UAT gap G-03-6: one floating window that is too big for the room must not float every docked window on its desk.

Root cause (binding, from 03-UAT.md G-03-6 artifacts and .planning/debug/g-03-6-fixed-size-window-overlap.md 16:03): `Planner.plan` sets the Desktop tile's minimum to the per-axis maximum of its floating windows' learned minimums. If that minimum leaves no room for the docked tiles, `resolve` makes every docked window unfit, and `Arranger.planFloatingLostRoom` floats them all. Plan 03-22 teaches Pane Reminders' 768 pt minimum. Once that window floats, its width (with Notes' 642 pt docked beside it on a 1024 pt display) would float Notes too. The UAT "missing" item asks to cap the Desktop minimum, or ignore floaters that can never fit.

Decision (Claude's discretion, within the orchestrator's directive): docked windows keep their tiles over a floater's minimum. Without zoom or collapsed tiles (Phase 4, ZOOM-13, deferred), a floater too big for the Desktop tile is clamped into it as far as its app allows and may still overlap a tile. That is the accepted cost (T-03-58). The old rule floated the whole desk instead.

Output: the Planner rule, Planner and Arranger tests, and a VM re-check.
</objective>
