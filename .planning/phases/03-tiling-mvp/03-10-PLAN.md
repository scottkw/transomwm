## Phase Goal

**As a** Mac user, **I want to** drag windows into nested tiles that reflow as windows open and close, **so that** my windows never overlap.

<objective>
Regroup the status menu into Arrange, Safety and Exclusions (UI-SPEC Surface 1), route every menu item through the same dispatcher as its hotkey (TILE-09), and add Pause/Resume (TILE-08), the Gaps preference (TILE-04) and Don't Arrange for the frontmost app with a hotkey (TILE-18).

Purpose: TILE-09's "menu items through one command dispatcher", plus three requirements that live in the menu. Later plans add their rows (Swap, Move, Snap, Split ▸ operations, Arrange All, Float All in plan 12; Restore All in plan 14; conflict lines and Restore macOS Window Settings in plan 16) to the same table.
Output: `CommandMenu`, regrouped `StatusItemController`, `MenuCopy` strings, `GapPreference`, pause reasons in the Arranger, the availability rule, two more hotkeys, tests.
</objective>
