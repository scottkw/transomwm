## Phase Goal

**As a** Mac user, **I want to** drag windows into nested tiles that reflow as windows open and close, **so that** my windows never overlap.

<objective>
Close UAT gap G-03-3: dragging the gap between two tiles should resize both smoothly, and the new split should stick.

Root cause (binding, from 03-UAT.md and .planning/debug/g-03-3-gap-drag.md). There are two independent bugs:
- H1, "won't drag one way". `Arranger.boundaryChange` converts the drag's points to weight against the laid-out length, then caps the move at what the shrinking side loses on screen. When the growing side is pinned at a minimum above its weight share, the pin absorbs the weight shift. With 2 children nothing moves; with 3 or more the wrong boundary moves, and an undo step is still recorded.
- H2, "goes back to the way it was". The live gap move exists only inside one `replan(with:on:)` call, whose `defer` restores the committed World. Any other `replan()` mid-drag re-writes the pre-drag tiles until the next drag event or mouse-up: a window event (Terminal's title change), an off-plan frame correction, or a correction tick.

Out of scope: the lower-confidence third contributor (pressWindow starting a DragSession on a window that spills into the gap) was not reproduced, and the edge-resize path's own mid-drag behaviour is not part of this gap.

Output: TreeEdits.setWeights, the re-based boundaryChange, Arranger liveGap state, tests, and a VM re-check of UAT test 3.
</objective>
