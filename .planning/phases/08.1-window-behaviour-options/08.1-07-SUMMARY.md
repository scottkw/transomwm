## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] `tart stop` undid a suite's restored guest state**
- **Found during:** Task 1, second VM run on macOS 27.2
- **Issue:** The test's `defer`s had run, but after `vm-session.sh` stopped the VM, `Application Support/Transom` and the `newWindows` key were back as they were mid-test. The next run then failed moving the folder aside. The likely cause is unflushed guest writes lost at the stop. I reasoned that from the timestamps and from the fix holding on every later run, and didn't isolate it further.
- **Fix:** `guest sync` before the session ends. I restored the 27.2 guest by hand once: the original folder from `Transom.before-options-test`, and both keys deleted.
- **Files modified:** `scripts/vm-session.sh`
- **Commit:** `9022979`

**2. [Test] The plan's plain 120 pt drag can't stay where it was released**
- **Found during:** Task 1
- **Issue:** Floating windows are clamped into the Desktop tile. Step 3 needs a drag just past the window's own width, as `HoldOptionIntegrationTests` already does. In step 4, window 1 can't fit: window 2 docks below the Desktop, which leaves it 329 pt of height for a 666 pt window.
- **Fix:** Each drag takes its travel from the caller. Step 3 moves window 0 past its width. Step 4 moves window 2 up 120 pt instead of dragging window 1, which still proves Hold ⌥ after the relaunch.
- **Files modified:** `Tests/TransomMacIntegrationTests/OptionsBundleIntegrationTests.swift`
- **Commit:** `9022979`

**3. [Test] The drag started before the neighbours' writes landed**

- **Fix:** One second's settle after the check.
- **Commit:** `9022979`

**4. [Test] Get Info's frame was read during its open animation**

- **Fix:** Read it one second after it appears.
