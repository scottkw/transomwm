## User notes
- During item 1 (2026-11-05): after relaunching with the apps from the last session closed, their waiting tiles covered the test Space until all four apps were opened, and each app's window then docked back into its old tile. Both match the current spec, read from the code and not reproduced: a waiting tile lasts until 5 min after Transom's launch if its app never opens (D-12, `Arranger.neverOpenedWait`), and re-matching isn't affected by New Windows or Drag to Dock (D-05). Open with the user: whether the wait or the way to dismiss these tiles should change.
- During item 3 (2026-11-05): dragging by rows, the user could only get top and bottom half or top and bottom quarter; the two middle quarters were highlighted but not selectable. Read from `LayoutTree.insertFourth` and `fourthShares`, not reproduced: when the root split runs the other way, the whole layout becomes one child of a new root. It can only sit on one side of the window, so D-14's no-holes rule grows the window into the empty side: 2nd row gives the top half, 3rd the bottom half, and the middle band the top three quarters. The spec allows this ("the new window grows"), but the bands and labels promise more. Open with the user.
- User decision (2026-11-05, "go with your picks"): add a Clear Waiting Tiles menu item, and keep the rows placement but make the labels and announcements say what the window actually gets.
- During item 5 (2026-11-05): after ⌃⌥⌘Z the auto-tiled window looked docked. It was floating where it had opened, over a tile; the tiles grew back.
- During item 8 (2026-11-05): with the Desktop tile showing, the desk was `[TextEdit | Desktop tile | Safari]` in thirds (read from layout.json). A 3rd-column drop left Safari too narrow; it most likely collapsed (the user saw it as minimized; not confirmed). Hiding the Desktop tile also hid floating FileZilla, and a new floating window showed the hidden tile again; both are designed behaviour (Phase 3), and neither is explained in the app.
- User decision (2026-11-05, "Yeah, let's get rid of it"): remove the Desktop tile. Inserted as Phase 08.1.1; item 8 is checked there.
