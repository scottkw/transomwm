## Phase Goal

**As a** new user, **I want to** install Transom with Homebrew and be guided through setup, **so that** it starts at login and keeps itself updated.

<objective>
Open one Settings window from the menu (⌘,) and move the Shortcuts window into it as the Shortcuts tab.

Later plans each add one entry: Arranging (09-04), Zoom & Gestures (09-05), Exclusions (09-08), General (09-09), Updates (09-11), Menu Bar (09-18, which also checks all seven are present).
