## Phase Goal

**As a** new user, **I want to** install Transom with Homebrew and be guided through setup, **so that** it starts at login and keeps itself updated.

<objective>
Close D-25 before v0.9.0 ships (09-27 and 09-28 run after this plan). 09-29 fixed one window that an app closes but keeps alive on macOS 27. Its "Not covered (a)" is still open: an app closes a whole native tab group and keeps the windows alive. CloseRule's tab-member check (`invalidElement` + CG present + known tab member gives `.state(.absent)`) and `decide`'s remaining-members check (members still in the CG list keep the group) both keep the group tracked, so its tile stays.

Measure first. Task 1 extends the fixture, which is test infrastructure: no existing command closes a whole tab group while keeping its windows alive. `close` releases its window, and Transom's AX close press closes one window. Task 1 then adds a live test that logs every tab's AX, CG and Space facts while the group is live and after the close, and runs it in both VMs. The facts must show that the closed group differs from a live one, or the plan stops with a CHECKPOINT.

The evidence the fix uses is group-level, because CG alone cannot tell a closed group's hidden members from live hidden tabs. A live group always has one selected tab: its element answers, and while its Space shows, AXWindows lists it. A closed group's shown tab answers -25202, and none of its tabs is listed or on screen. WindowTracker confirms that state twice, `settleDelay` (250 ms) apart, before treating the group as closed. So a Cmd-W whose next tab AppKit has not listed yet never loses a live group. It acts only while the group's own Space shows, the Spaces lookup is on and the app is not hidden. Under those conditions a live group's selected tab would be listed. Once confirmed, the shown tab still needs its own CG evidence through CloseRule (absent, no Space, or ordered out on a shown Space). This keeps the two-source rule (D-09, WIN-09) for the group window.

Purpose: Core Value. Windows must stay correctly arranged as they close. On macOS 27 a closed tab group must not keep its tile.
Output: two fixture commands, two live tests with logged facts, the WindowTracker confirmation and its engine tests (written to fail first), live proof in both VMs, a deferred-items record and a release-notes line.
</objective>
