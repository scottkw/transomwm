## Phase Goal

**As a** multi-display user, **I want to** see every window on every display and Space in one overview, **so that** I can jump anywhere and keep each layout.

<objective>
Selection and keyboard (UI-SPEC Surface 3, D-04): one shared selection across displays, arrow/Tab/Return/Esc keys through temporary Carbon hotkeys, pointer hover selection, picking a same-Space window, and the selected-window title capsule.

Purpose: OVER-01's "clicking a window goes to it" needs a keyboard equivalent for accessibility parity, and pointer and keyboard must never fight.

Output: `OverviewSelection` (pure), key and hover wiring in `OverviewController`, the ring and title capsule, a VM live test.
</objective>
