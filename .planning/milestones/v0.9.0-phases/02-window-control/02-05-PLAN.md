## Phase Goal

**As a** Mac user, **I want to** grant Pane access and have it track and move every window, **so that** no slow app or lock screen can freeze or lose them.

<objective>

Purpose: WIN-01 and D-02 (ROADMAP SC1).
Output: PermissionReducer (+ tests), the Permission/ folder in PaneMac, TCCReset tests, menu and app wiring.
</objective>
