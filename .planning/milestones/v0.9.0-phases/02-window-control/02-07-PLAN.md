## Phase Goal

**As a** Mac user, **I want to** grant Pane access and have it track and move every window, **so that** no slow app or lock screen can freeze or lose them.

<objective>
Make a frozen or slow app harmless: detect when an app stops answering AX, stop sending it work except a cheap periodic probe, report it, and recover automatically, while every other app keeps being tracked at full speed.

Purpose: WIN-03 (ROADMAP SC4, first half) and the D-16 unresponsiveness tracker, plus D-14's hung/slow-app fake scenarios.
Output: ResponsivenessTracker (+ tests), in-flight coalescing and degraded handling in AXWindowSystem, the fake's hung/slow simulation, a SIGSTOP integration test.
</objective>

AXWindowSystem: keep a ResponsivenessTracker. Map each observation to a JobOutcome (`listFailure == .cannotComplete` → cannotComplete; other listFailure → otherFailure; else success), call `responsiveness.record(...)`, emit `.appResponsiveness` on change, and put `responsiveness.degraded` in `inventory().degradedPIDs`.
