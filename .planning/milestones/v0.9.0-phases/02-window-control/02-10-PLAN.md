## Phase Goal

**As a** Mac user, **I want to** grant Pane access and have it track and move every window, **so that** no slow app or lock screen can freeze or lose them.

<objective>
Count a natively tabbed window as one window: Pane recognises AppKit tab groups from the visible tab's AXTabGroup plus its hidden CG siblings, keeps one stable identity for the group, and follows the visible tab without ever reporting tab switches as closes and opens.

Purpose: WIN-06 and D-08 (ROADMAP SC2's "a natively tabbed Finder, Terminal or Safari window counts as one").
Output: TabGroupTracker (+ tests), group handling in WindowTracker and the fake, a fixture integration test.
</objective>

    - visible id 10 with tabCount 3 and CG siblings 11, 12 (same pid, layer 0, offscreen, identical bounds) → one group, leader 10, members {10, 11, 12}, tabCount 3; groupFor[10] == leader
    - next update: visible id 11 with tabCount 3, 10 now offscreen → same group, groupFor[11] == leader 10 (rebinding), no new group
    - sibling bounds off by exactly 1.0 pt on x → member; off by 1.01 pt → not a member

  <action>
Per D-08 and RESEARCH Pattern 3. Tests first (RED), then implementation (GREEN). Groups are kept per pid. Membership compares each Rect side with `abs(a - b) <= boundsTolerance`. A visible id that is already a member of a group binds to that group; otherwise, a visible window with tabCount ≥ 2 whose computed members intersect an existing group joins it; otherwise it starts a new group with itself as leader. Members are recomputed from the CG list on every update (hidden tabs are CG-only: no AX element, no title), but the leader never changes while any member remains. Doc comment: identity is the leader's ref for the group's life even after the leader tab closes; Phases 3-4 must address the window through `visibleMemberID`.

  <action>
Per D-08. Scenario tests first (RED), then implementation (GREEN).

WindowTracker.ingest: before upserting, collect the listed visible windows (id, frame, snapshot tabCount) and call `tabGroups.update(pid:bundleID:visible:cgWindows:)`. Each listed window resolves to its group identity (`groupFor[id]`, or itself when ungrouped): upsert the WindowInfo under that identity with `visibleMemberID = id`, the tab's frame and title, and `tabCount` from the resolution; a change of visibleMemberID alone emits `.windowFocused(groupRef)` and never created/destroyed. Hidden members are never separate WindowInfos. Run the close decisions after rebinding, for group identities whose current visible member was not resolved this refresh; pass `isKnownTabMember: tabGroups.isKnownMember(id, pid:)`; when the visible member closes and another member exists, keep the group and let the next listed tab rebind it; destroy the group only when no member remains (call `memberClosed` as members leave the CG list).

FakeWindowSystem: a `tabGroup(ids:frame:pid:visible:)` helper that creates members with the same frame, the visible one listed and onscreen with `snapshot.tabGroup = TabGroupFact(tabCount: n)`, the others unlisted, offscreen, in the CG list; `selectTab(_ id:)` flips listed/onScreen flags.

TabsIntegrationTests (`.serialized`, `.enabled(if: IntegrationEnv.trusted, ...)`, `@MainActor`, `.timeLimit(.minutes(1))`): fixture `--window tabs:3`; expect exactly one inventory window for the fixture pid with tabCount 3 whose visibleMemberID is one of the three printed ids; record its identity; `send("select-tab 1")` (pick an index whose id differs from the visible one) → within 1 s visibleMemberID equals that tab's id, identity unchanged, and no windowCreated/windowDestroyed for the fixture pid in that interval; `send("close <visible index>")` → tabCount 2 within 1 s, identity unchanged.
