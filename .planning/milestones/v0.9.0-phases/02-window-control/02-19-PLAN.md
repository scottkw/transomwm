## Phase Goal

**As a** Mac user, **I want to** grant Transom access and have it track and move every window, **so that** no slow app or lock screen can freeze or lose them.

<objective>
Close UAT gap G-02-10 (WIN-06, D-08). A native tab group must be one tracked window: new tabs, tab switches and Cmd-W change its title and tab count, and never add or leave extra rows. In the VM, Transom tracked 15 windows while 13 existed, and the extra rows never cleared.

Root cause (`.planning/debug/g-02-10-tab-group-stale-rows.md`, reproduced live as E1 and E4): TabGroupTracker recognises a hidden tab only as an offscreen layer-0 CG entry whose bounds equal the shown tab's AX frame within 1 pt (`slot`, `matches`). The Cmd-T opener, the room-rule join, the orphan handoff and the fold all depend on that slot. On macOS 15.7.7 a hidden tab keeps the CG bounds it had when it was hidden, and AppKit gives it the group's frame only when it shows it again. So when the group frame changes, the slot misses the tab shown just before, and the new tab founds a second group. Two things make that frame change: Terminal's first Cmd-T adds a 28 pt tab bar, and a resized Finder tab hands Cmd-T a sibling's frame. CloseRule keeps the old identity forever ("valid, unlisted, in CG" is noChange), and group leaders are never folded. FakeWindowSystem puts every tab at the group's current frame, so no test could see this.

The fix has two parts:
- Fake fidelity: a new tab can open at its own frame, a shown tab takes the group frame, and hidden tabs keep their last shown frame.
- In TabGroupTracker, link a newly shown tab to the tab shown just before it on timing evidence the OS keeps current: that tab was listed at the app's last update, is unlisted now, and is still offscreen in the CG list, AX-valid, and not on a known different Space. A group also keeps its own hidden members while that holds. Bounds stay a tie-break and still find the other hidden tabs. With two candidates, the nearest by origin and width wins.

CloseRule and WindowTracker do not change. CloseRule's noChange for a valid, unlisted, in-CG window is what keeps hidden tabs and other-Space windows alive (WIN-09). The fix stops the leftover identity from being created in the first place.

Purpose: Phase 3 tiles follow a tracked identity. An extra row is a phantom tile, and a group founded twice splits one window's tile in two.
Output: fake fidelity with its unit tests, failing-first scenario tests for E1, E4, Cmd-W and a frame write, unit tests for every new tracker rule, and the TabGroupTracker change with its doc comment.
</objective>

SUMMARY. Known limits:
- A Cmd-T or a tab switch in the same update as a Space switch without Space data is linked only through the slot. A stale-bounds tab there can still leave a row until the app relaunches. This is rare, and it errs the safe way: no merge, no destroy.
- Two windows of one app going hidden in one refresh are told apart by the slot, then by origin and width.
