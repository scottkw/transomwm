## Phase Goal

**As a** Mac user, **I want to** grant Pane access and have it track and move every window, **so that** no slow app or lock screen can freeze or lose them.

<objective>
Turn the tracer into a real app the user can launch: a menu-bar status item whose menu shows what Pane is tracking, built as a Developer-ID-signed `Pane Debug.app` (and `Pane.app` in Release) by XcodeGen around the SwiftPM targets, launchable through LaunchServices with `--disable-private`.

Purpose: D-01 (menu-bar shell), D-03/D-04/D-05 (signing, bundle IDs, generated project) so TCC grants survive rebuilds (WIN-01's stale-grant story starts with not causing it), and the SAFE-05 launch check. Later plans add menu items (05 permission, 08 tracked windows/exclusions/freeze, 13 nudge).
Output: StatusItemController, MenuCopy (+ tests), `xcode/project.yml`, `xcode/Pane.entitlements`, three scripts, `.gitignore` entries.
</objective>
