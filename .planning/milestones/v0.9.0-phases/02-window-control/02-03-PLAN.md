## Phase Goal

**As a** Mac user, **I want to** grant Pane access and have it track and move every window, **so that** no slow app or lock screen can freeze or lose them.

<objective>
Teach Pane which windows it may ever arrange. A pure classifier decides dockable vs never-dock from an AX snapshot plus the CG window layer; the tracker applies it with the settle delay and stickiness rules; the live system gains the CG window list as its second source; and a `pane-dump` tool records a redacted, developer-labelled corpus of real apps' windows that the classifier is tested against.

Purpose: WIN-05 and D-06/D-07. Dialogs, sheets, popovers, panels and picture-in-picture windows are tracked but never docked.
Output: Classifier, CorpusCase, CGWindowList, pane-dump, the AX-dump corpus with its tests, and classification in the live tracker.
</objective>
