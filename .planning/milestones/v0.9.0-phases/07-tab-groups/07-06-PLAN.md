## Phase Goal

**As a** Mac user, **I want to** group windows from any apps into one tabbed tile, **so that** related windows share space without overlapping.

<objective>
TABS-03's dock/float half and the whole-group rule of UI-SPEC Surface 5: every path that moves "the focused window" moves the whole group when that window is a group member (RESEARCH Pattern 9, Pitfall 9). The model gains whole-leaf edits; the engine branches each dock/float/drag/swap/rehome/arrange path; the strip gains its Float Group / Dock Group button.

Purpose: without these, dragging or floating a group's window would tear one member out and could leave members overlapping; D-06 requires the dock/float button to act on every window.

Output: `removeLeaf`/`insertLeaf`/`applyDrop(leaf)`, relaxed `swap`, `removeGroup`/`addGroup`, engine whole-group branches, `TabAction.toggleGroupFloat`, the strip's third button.
</objective>
