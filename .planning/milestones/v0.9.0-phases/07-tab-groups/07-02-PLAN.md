## Phase Goal

**As a** Mac user, **I want to** group windows from any apps into one tabbed tile, **so that** related windows share space without overlapping.

<objective>
TABS-02's slice: groups get their tab strip, and a click on a tab swaps the visible window with no wallpaper flash. The engine emits pure `TabStrip` values and runs the reveal-then-park state machine; the shell draws pooled strip panels (CollapsedPanels recipe at `.normal` level, bound to its own Space) and feeds clicks back. Activation of a hidden member another way (D-08) selects it, guarded against the focus fights of Pitfall 6.

Purpose: without a strip the group from 07-01 has no way to show its hidden member. This plan makes a group usable end to end; 07-03 completes the strip's geometry, accessibility, following and Space handling.

Output: `TabStrip`/`GroupKey`/`TabAction`/`PendingReveal` in Arranger+Tabs.swift, `.tabStrips` and `.raiseStrip` effects, `ArrangerInput.tab`, `TabStripPanels.swift`, strip press routing in ArrangeController, TabCopy additions, a second VM test.
</objective>
