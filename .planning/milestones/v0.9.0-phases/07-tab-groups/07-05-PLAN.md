## Phase Goal

**As a** Mac user, **I want to** group windows from any apps into one tabbed tile, **so that** related windows share space without overlapping.

<objective>
TABS-03's close and minimise half: the strip's Close Group and Minimize Group buttons act on the whole group. Two new AX commands (close press, minimise) run on each app's AX thread; the engine sequences Close Group one member at a time with a deadline reconciled against Transom's two-source close confirmation (RESEARCH Pitfall 10, A4), and handles members closing on their own (D-12 neighbour, dissolve). Two VM tests prove the AX commands and the same-app minimise case.

Purpose: D-06 requires the group's buttons to act on every window while still letting apps protect unsaved work.

Output: `WindowCommand.close`/`.minimize`, AX jobs, `CloseQueue`, minimise flow, neighbour reveal on close, dissolve copy, Close/Minimize buttons on the strip, two VM tests.
</objective>
