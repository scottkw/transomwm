## Phase Goal

**As a** Mac user, **I want to** group windows from any apps into one tabbed tile, **so that** related windows share space without overlapping.

<objective>
The phase tracer: a docked window dropped on the middle of another docked window, after a 400 ms rest, becomes a tab group. The model reserves the 32 pt band and hands the engine one frame (selected) plus one sized park (hidden); the engine runs the group dwell, the group dry run and the one-undo-step commit; the shell parks at the given size, draws the Group preview and speaks the announcement. A VM test proves a real hidden member parks at the content size.

Purpose: RESEARCH identified three structural gaps (band missing from `resolve`, parks cannot set a size, centre drops refused). Closing them on one real path first proves the architecture before strips, floating groups and tear-off build on it.

Output: `TabBand`, the band in `resolve`, `WindowCommand.park(size:)`, sized parks in Planner/ViewPlanner/planShown, group dwell + `PreviewState.group`, `Arranger+Tabs.swift`, `TabCopy`, Group preview drawing, `TabGroupIntegrationTests` (first test).
</objective>
