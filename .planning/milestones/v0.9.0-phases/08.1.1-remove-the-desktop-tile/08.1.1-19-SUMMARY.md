### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 3 - Blocking] Step 5's grab point was covered by Transom's own conflict alert**
- **Found during:** Task 1, first 27.2 run.
- **Issue:** `LiveArrange.titleBar` refused window 1 at (512, 50): "not topmost … 71 pid 854". Two diagnostic runs (`.build/0811-19-t1-vm27-diag.log`, `.build/0811-19-t1-vm27-diag2.log`) showed that `order-back` worked, since windows 0 and 2 went behind window 1. The window covering the grab point was an untitled Transom Debug window, 410×509 at (307, 36). That is `ConflictController.showModelessAlert`, which calls `alert.window.center()`. It appears at each launch because the VMs keep macOS edge tiling on, which HoldOption needs. This behaviour dates from Phase 3 (SAFE-04) and is intended, not a regression. The old step 5 never hit it because it grabbed a narrow first-column tile.
- **Fix:** test code only. The private `plainDrag` gained a defaulted `grabbingAt along: Double = 0.5`, so existing call sites are unchanged. Step 5 grabs window 1 at 7/8 of its width, clear of the centred alert, and still drags it left by a quarter. The order-backs stay, as the plan specified. The alert is not dismissed, because "Not Now" would write `conflictNotNow` into the guest's defaults, which `Guest.restore` does not put back.
- **Files modified:** Tests/TransomMacIntegrationTests/OptionsBundleIntegrationTests.swift
- **Commit:** e65a4d7 (the temporary diagnostic prints were removed before the commit)

**Total deviations:** 1 auto-fixed (Rule 3). **Impact:** test-only. The plan said to keep the helpers' signatures, and `plainDrag`'s call sites are unchanged. No product behaviour changed.
