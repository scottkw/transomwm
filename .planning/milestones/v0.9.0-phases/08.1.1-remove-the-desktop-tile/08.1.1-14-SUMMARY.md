# Phase 08.1.1 Plan 14: Engine plans and signatures without the Desktop rect Summary

**`FramePlan` and `ViewPlan` no longer have `desktopRect`, and the planners no longer take the unread `wanted:` frames. Snap, Float and Move Out of Group now take the visible frame as `area:`, and their frames are unchanged.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** about 15 min
- **Started:** 2026-11-06
- **Completed:** 2026-11-06
- **Tasks:** 2
- **Files modified:** 15

## Accomplishments

- `FramePlan.desktopRect` and `ViewPlan.desktopRect` are deleted, along with their initialiser arguments.
- The unread `wanted:` parameter is gone from `Planner.plan`, `ViewPlanner.plan` and `ViewPlanner.collapsing`. Eleven engine call sites no longer pass `wantedFrames`: Arranger (2), Arranger+Camera (4), Arranger+Placeholders (1), Arranger+Tabs (1) and Navigator (3).
- The visible frame is now labelled `area:` in four signatures, each with a doc that says so:
  - `Snap.frame(_:window:area:gap:)`
  - `Planner.floatFrame(wanted:tile:area:minimum:)`
  - `Planner.clamp(_:into area:)` (the call label `into:` is unchanged)
  - `Arranger.moveOutFrame(size:group:area:)`
- Desktop-tile wording is gone from the docs in Snap, Planner, Arranger+Tabs (`tabStrips`) and Arranger+Commands (`moveOrSnap`, `largestTile`, `Edge.half`).
- In the tests, SnapTests' file-level `desktop` constant is now `area`, and ArrangerTabsMoveOutTests' is `visible`. Three PlannerTests names now say "VisibleFrame" instead of "Desktop". No frame expectation changed.
