- Deleted `Node.desktop`, `NodeID.desktop`, `withDesktop()`, `isDesktopHidden`, `setDesktopHidden(_:)`, `LayoutError.nothingDocked` and `.desktopTile`. `removeLeaf` no longer re-shows the Desktop, and the compiler listed every Desktop arm, all removed.
