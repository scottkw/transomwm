## Phase Goal

**As a** user, **I want to** have docked windows fill the whole screen and floating windows sit on top of them like normal macOS windows, **so that** I never have to learn or manage a "Desktop tile".

<objective>
Put the corner quarters on the hotkey and menu routes (8.1.1 D-17): four rebindable chords, a Corners section in the Dock Window submenu, and four rows in the Shortcuts window.

Chord choice (Claude's discretion, checked against Hotkeys.swift at HEAD 200dde9). The ⇧⌃⌥⌘ defaults in use are the four arrows, D, F, T, 0-9 and Y U H J I O P K L ;. The corners take ⇧⌃⌥⌘ with `-` `=` over `[` `]`, a 2×2 block right after the fourths' digits: top row = top corners, left key = left corner. A fumbled ⇧ runs ⌃⌥⌘- Shrink Tile, ⌃⌥⌘= Grow Tile (both undoable), or ⌃⌥⌘[ / ] Zoom Back / Forward (camera only). All are safe. U I J K stay with the halves and thirds, and the floating snaps keep ⌃⌥⌘U/I/J/K. 08.1.1-26 checks the four chords against macOS in both VMs.

Purpose: D-17 asks for corners "on every route"; 08.1.1-23 did URL and CLI.
Output: four `KeyboardShortcuts.Name`s, the Corners menu section, four Shortcuts rows.
</objective>
