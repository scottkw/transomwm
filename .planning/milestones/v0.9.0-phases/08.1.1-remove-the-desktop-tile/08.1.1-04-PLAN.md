## Phase Goal

**As a** user, **I want to** have docked windows fill the whole screen and floating windows sit on top of them like normal macOS windows, **so that** I never have to learn or manage a "Desktop tile".

<objective>
Delete Hide/Show Desktop Tile (D-09) and Frame Desktop (D-13) themselves: the `Command` and `CameraCommand` cases, their Arranger and Navigator handlers, their `transom://` and CLI names and help rows, and the `MenuCopy` arms that named them; and reword the New Windows Float tooltip.

Purpose: after 08.1.1-02 (behaviour tests) and 08.1.1-03 (shell routes), only the engine, URL and CLI still know the two commands. Deleting the cases now lets the compiler confirm nothing reaches them, and leaves every hidden-Desktop engine path unreachable for 08.1.1-06 to 08.1.1-08 to delete.

Output: smaller `Command`/`CameraCommand`, URL table and help; Arranger and Navigator without the two handlers; MenuCopy without their titles; updated tests.
</objective>
