# Phase 8.1.1 Plan 03: Take the Desktop tile commands out of the shell Summary

**Hide/Show Desktop Tile and Frame Desktop no longer appear in the status menu, the hotkey table or the Shortcuts window. A wallpaper double-click now goes straight to macOS. The toasts say "floating" instead of "on the Desktop".**

## Accomplishments

- Hide/Show Desktop Tile is gone from the Arrange section, `CommandMenu.commands`, `ZoomCopy.shortcutTitle` and the Shortcuts window, and ⌃⌥⌘H is no longer bound (D-09)
- Frame Desktop is gone from the Zoom section (item and checkmark), the hotkey table and the Shortcuts window, and ⌃⌥⌘G is no longer bound. `zoomSection` lost its `framingDesktop:` parameter, and Replace Mission Control now follows Frame Focused Window (D-13)
- `ZoomInput` no longer handles a wallpaper double-click: `desktopDoubleClick(at:)`, `finderElement(at:pid:)`, `isDesktopItem(_:)` and `ZoomCopy.logDesktopDoubleClick` are deleted. Title-bar double-click is unchanged
- The `toggleDesktop` and `frameDesktop` raw values are retired in a comment and checked by `theRetiredDesktopNamesAreNotInTheTable` (T-08.1.1-06)
- The Shortcuts window lists 74 rows in 9 sections (Zoom has 11), and `Hotkeys.entries` has 74 entries
- The lost-room, arrange-leftovers and crash-recovery toasts end in "floating." (UI-SPEC Copywriting Contract)
