# Phase 08.1.1 Plan 21: Exact halves, thirds and fourths Summary

**Every docking position now gives exactly its span. With three floating windows and nothing docked, Left Half takes only the left half and the right half stays empty (UAT round 2 issue 1). Empty space is used before any docked window shrinks, nothing grows, a root split the other way gives up its empty margins first, and a docked window moved to a position leaves its old place empty. Labels name the position. "Whole desk", "two thirds" and "three quarters" are gone.**

## Accomplishments

- `insertFourth` (8.1.1 D-16, E1-E3). The root's children along the axis become pieces: tiles, empty lengths, and children not taking part. Empty areas are cut by the window's span. Tiles go before or after by the existing rule (a == 0, b == 1, otherwise by centre with ties after). Each side then fits its room: empty first, then tiles shrink proportionally. A side that is too short gets an empty piece next to the window. Empty pieces under `minWeight` are dropped, the split is normalised and validated, and it all commits or nothing does. A root at `maxChildren` still throws `splitFull`.
- Cross-axis roots (E2, the D-17 corner case). `margins(of:axis:)` and `trimMargins(of:axis:lead:trail:)` work on any node. A leaf or cross-split root is one child whose empty margins along the axis become empty pieces. Rows `[(X | _) / (Y | _)]` + Right Half gives `[(X / Y) .5 | W .5]`, and X and Y keep their size.
- E4 in `Arranger.dropping`: for a `.fourth` target on the same desk, a docked panel's leaf or a docked group's leaf is vacated instead of removed. Cross-desk moves and other targets keep today's removal (E5).
- `FourthCopy.label(_:_:span:)` and `LayoutTree.fourthSpan` deleted. `nameFourth` became one line, `FourthCopy.label(slot, axis)`.
