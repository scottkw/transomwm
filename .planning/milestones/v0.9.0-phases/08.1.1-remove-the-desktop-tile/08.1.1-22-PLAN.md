## Phase Goal

**As a** user, **I want to** have docked windows fill the whole screen and floating windows sit on top of them like normal macOS windows, **so that** I never have to learn or manage a "Desktop tile".

<objective>
Make empty areas behave as empty screen everywhere else in the engine (8.1.1 D-16, rules E6 and E7 from 08.1.1-20-PLAN.md). After 08.1.1-21, docking positions leave empty areas. Without this plan, those areas would act as invisible tiles: drags would show split regions over them, Dock Where It Is would split them, New Windows: Tile could split one, and focus or zoom could land on one. D-16's "until another window docks into it" needs the ordinary ways in: the screen-edge drag, ⌃⌥⌘D, Tile, Arrange All and the Finder split.

Purpose: the empty part of the screen stays empty until the user docks into it, and nothing treats it as a window.
Output: `DropTarget.fill`, the edge-band rule for empty areas, a fill-first placement rule for Tile, Arrange All and Finder, and empty areas skipped by focus, move and zoom.
</objective>
