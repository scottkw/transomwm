## Fixed Issues

### WR-01: `autoTile` runs during a live drag

**Status:** fixed: requires human verification
**Files modified:** `Sources/TransomEngine/Arranger.swift`, `Sources/TransomEngine/Arranger+NewWindows.swift`, `Tests/TransomEngineTests/ArrangerNewWindowsTests.swift`
**Commit:** 99dcbde
**Applied fix:** A window that opens during a press or gap drag stays on no desk until the gesture ends, then **floats**. It does not auto-tile.

The review's one-line guard was not enough. A floating window can still show a hidden Desktop, or resize the Desktop for its minimum, and either moves tiles under the drag. So `admit` holds every window while `gestureLive` is true, and `handle` admits the held ones once the gesture ends. The tests compare the drag's committed weights with and without the window: resize drag, resize drag with a hidden Desktop, and gap drag.

### WR-05: A new window whose minimum isn't known yet

**Status:** fixed: requires human verification, with a known limit
**Files modified:** `Sources/TransomEngine/Arranger.swift`, `Sources/TransomEngine/Arranger+NewWindows.swift`, `Tests/TransomEngineTests/ArrangerNewWindowsTests.swift`
**Commit:** bafefdf
**Applied fix:** Auto-tiled windows with no known minimum are tracked until a read-back fits. If a learned minimum then leaves the desk without room, D-06's outcome applies:
- **Zoom off:** the window floats where it opened, with no notice.
- **Zoom on:** it collapses, with the usual collapse announcement.

A read-back that fits ends the tracking, so a later loss of room gets the ordinary notice.

**Known limit** (also a `shortcut:` comment in the code): macOS can't report a minimum before docking, so the first window of such an app still shows in the tile for about a second. Its undo step also stays, and undoing it changes nothing visible. Curing both needs a probe, which was ruled out.

## Behaviour a user would notice

- A window opening during a drag stays out of the layout until the drag ends, then floats, even in Tile mode.
- Undoing an auto-tile on a hidden Desktop brings the window back on screen where it opened.
- In Tile mode, the first new window after launch lands beside the focused tile.
- A tie in move or focus after launch can go to the window focused at launch.
- With Tile on, a first window that turns out too big floats with no toast, or collapses with zoom on.
- Fourth guides stay through a display change during a drag.
- Dropping a window on its own zone no longer says "Docked" or adds an undo step.
- As URLs, dock commands run at most one per second across all ten. The CLI is unchanged.
- `vm-session.sh` refuses a VM that is already running, instead of stopping it.

## Residual risks

- **A stale gesture holds windows back.** After a lost mouse-up, a new window stays on no desk until the next press. After a lost gap-drag end, it waits until the next gap drag ends. Both recover on their own, and the window can't be dragged until then.
- **`scripts/vm-spike.sh` has the same empty-IP gap.** It was outside the scope given, so it is unchanged. The guard is the same three lines.
