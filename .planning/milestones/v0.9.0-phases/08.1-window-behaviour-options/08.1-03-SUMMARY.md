**`Command.dockFourth` takes a share of the whole desk at the root, by D-14's exact arithmetic, in one undo step, with zoom-aware refusal and collapse, and ten rebindable ⇧⌃⌥⌘ digit chords listed in a new Shortcuts section.**

## Accomplishments

- A fourth docks a floating window, or moves a docked one, to a span of the desk. Tiles centred before it go before it, the rest go after, and an empty side is filled by the window. A hidden or absent child keeps its stored weight and takes no part in the position.
- A root split the other way becomes one child of a new root. A tree too full or too deep throws through `process`'s existing catch, with nothing changed.
- The command refuses, collapses, beeps on no change and widens a zoomed camera as UI-SPEC Surface 4 says. `commit` is untouched for its callers.
- `FourthZones` and `DropTarget.fourth` are ready for 08.1-05's drags, and `Command.dockFourth` for 08.1-04's URL, CLI and menu routes.
