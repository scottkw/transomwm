## Phase Goal

**As a** Mac user, **I want to** drag windows into nested tiles that reflow as windows open and close, **so that** my windows never overlap.

<objective>
Phase tracer: prove the tiling architecture end to end on real fixture windows before anything else is built on it. A pure `Arranger` in PaneEngine owns the World; a thin MainActor `ArrangeController` in PaneMac becomes the only reader of `system.events`, runs the Arranger's effects through `system.perform`, and fans the inventory out to the menu. Dock, Float and Undo work from ⌃⌥⌘D/F/Z; new windows float on the Desktop tile; closing, minimising or fullscreening a docked window reflows its neighbours, and the window comes back to its tile.

Purpose: TILE-03, TILE-07, TILE-09 (core commands through one dispatcher), TILE-10, TILE-20 and WIN-07. Every later plan extends the contract fixed here.
Output: Arranger, Planner, Command, ArrangeController, Hotkeys (dock/float/undo), the KeyboardShortcuts 3.1.0 dependency, fixture fullscreen commands, fake fullscreen helper, tests.
</objective>
