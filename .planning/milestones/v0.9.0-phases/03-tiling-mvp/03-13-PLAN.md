## Phase Goal

**As a** Mac user, **I want to** drag windows into nested tiles that reflow as windows open and close, **so that** my windows never overlap.

<objective>
The green button toggles a window between docked and floating (TILE-06) while ⌥-click keeps the normal macOS zoom (TILE-19), and docked windows resize by their own edges or by dragging the gap between them (TILE-05).

Purpose: the remaining pointer interactions of success criteria 1, 2 and 7, without AX calls in the event-tap callback.
Output: green-button frame reads, the tap's swallow rule, Arranger toggles, edge-resize sessions, gap-handle geometry and panels, tests.
</objective>
