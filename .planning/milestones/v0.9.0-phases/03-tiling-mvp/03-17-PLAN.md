## Phase Goal

**As a** Mac user, **I want to** drag windows into nested tiles that reflow as windows open and close, **so that** my windows never overlap.

<objective>
Build 0.3.0, the first usable release (D-30), as a local artifact: a local `scripts/release.sh` that builds a Developer ID-signed, hardened, securely timestamped, notarised and stapled Pane.app and DMG, and its first real run, which also proves that a hardened build loading SkyLight at runtime notarises.

Purpose: ROADMAP success criterion 5 and the TILE-10 check on the installed build. CI notarisation and the Homebrew cask are Phase 9.
Output: `scripts/release.sh`, version and timestamp settings in `xcode/project.yml`, `build/` ignored, a notarised and stapled local `build/Pane-0.3.0.dmg` whose sha256 is recorded in the SUMMARY.

Note (lead decision 2026-10-25): after Phase 3 the app is renamed from Pane to Transom (modules, repo and folder), and nothing may be published publicly under "Pane". The 0.3.0 public pre-release is therefore held until the Transom rename; this plan creates no GitHub release, upload or tag. This supersedes the "GitHub pre-release uploaded by hand" part of D-30.
</objective>
