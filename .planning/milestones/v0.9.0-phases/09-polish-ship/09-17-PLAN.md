## Phase Goal

**As a** new user, **I want to** install Transom with Homebrew and be guided through setup, **so that** it starts at login and keeps itself updated.

<objective>
Tell the user about an update without interrupting them (a dot on the icon and an "Update to…" item, no notification), check daily on its own, and give them a one-click way to report a problem that never leaks what's on their screen (SHIP-07, SHIP-04 automatic check, D-09, D-10, D-15).

Output: the dot composite and update menu items, the automatic schedule and its Debug line, `ReportProblem` and its menu item.
</objective>
