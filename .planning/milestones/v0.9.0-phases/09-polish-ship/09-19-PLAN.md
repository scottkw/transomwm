## Phase Goal

**As a** new user, **I want to** install Transom with Homebrew and be guided through setup, **so that** it starts at login and keeps itself updated.

<objective>
Grow the Set Up Transom window into a short first-run setup: Welcome → Accessibility (Phase 2's step, unchanged) → Done, driven by a pure step function in TransomEngine (D-05, D-06, SHIP-02, UI-SPEC Surface 2, RESEARCH Pattern 5).

09-20 adds Screen Recording and Done's Open Settings; 09-21 the macOS-features and launch-at-login steps, Back and resume; 09-22 Move to Applications. Each adds its step to `OnboardingFlow.steps` with its view.
Output: `OnboardingFlow`, `OnboardingCopy`, `OnboardingView` with Welcome and Done, the first-run gate.
</objective>
