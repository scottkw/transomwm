## Phase Goal

**As a** trackpad user, **I want to** pinch and pan the desk with my fingers, **so that** zooming and moving around feels direct.

<objective>

Purpose: D-01 and D-03 are the feel of the phase, and this is where gestures first move the camera. The idle-camera case is completed here. Gestures with no target (overscroll, refusals) and gestures that start during a move (E-03) are plan 05-05.

Output: follow mode in ZoomController and StagePanel, GestureController's recognizer wiring and outcomes, VM integration tests, headless follow-math tests.
</objective>
