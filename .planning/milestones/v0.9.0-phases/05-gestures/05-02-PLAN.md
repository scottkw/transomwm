## Phase Goal

**As a** trackpad user, **I want to** pinch and pan the desk with my fingers, **so that** zooming and moving around feels direct.

<objective>

Purpose: D-08 puts all gesture judgement where the VM's missing trackpad doesn't matter: headless tests over recorded and synthetic traces. D-02 and E-03 need a preview that matches the eventual commit tile for tile.

Output: GestureRecognizer.swift, the previewMove/gestureTarget split in Arranger+Camera.swift, trace fixtures and tests.

GestureRecognizer.swift (new, TransomEngine, `import TransomModel` only, no AppKit or CoreGraphics). The types listed under "Artifacts this phase produces". This task covers the pinch half end-to-end:
- `Sample` fields: t (seconds), kind, phase (Int, CG encoding: 1 began, 2 changed, 4 ended, 8 cancelled, 128 mayBegin), momentum (Int: 0 none, 1 begin, 2 continue, 3 end), magnification, dx, dy.
- `feed` clamps each magnification to ±`magnificationClamp` and treats NaN/∞ as 0. It accumulates s = 1 + Σm (floored at 0.05) and computes p = ln(s)/ln 2.
- It emits `.started` once |ln s| ≥ `pinchDeadZone`, then `.progress` on every sample. Direction is `.pinchIn` for p > 0, `.pinchOut` for p < 0; crossing zero emits `.reversed`.
- Gesture phase 4 or 8 emits `.ended(Release)`. passedThreshold = |p| ≥ `pinchCommit`, or a flick (rate over the last `flickWindow` seconds ≥ `pinchFlickRate` with |p| ≥ `pinchFlickMinimum`, in the direction of travel). tooSmall = never started. Samples after `.ended` are ignored.
- `follow(hasTarget:)` implements the UI-SPEC Surface 1 tables. With a target, t = min(|p|, 1) and overscroll past 1; with none, t = 0 and overscroll from 0. overscrollScale = 2^(±R(x, `pinchOverscrollLimit`)), and `static func overscroll(_ x: Double, limit L: Double) -> Double` returns L·(1 − 1/(x·`overscrollCoefficient`/L + 1)).
